    4 · Results in simulation

    Plan + motion uses Sprout's own model (25 kg, 1.13 m) and grades the plan plus Astra's whole-body motion: contacts, support, balance, penetration and end pose. Plan only grades just the plan, for a stand-in robot (45 kg, 0.70 m hand reach). Astra named the right object or place on every command in both runs.
